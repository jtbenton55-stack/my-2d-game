# Creative Direction

## Thesis
This is a polished, personal top-down heist roguelite about **Parmida**, a stylish Persian-American woman chasing "crime lord" status, and **Bentley**, her aloof black Shiba Inu. She is not a bad person and not a dangerous criminal — she wants the *legend*: the style, the respect, the name whispered in Nocturne City. Every "crime" she pulls turns out to be a favor; the syndicate she builds turns out to be a friend group. In chasing the title, she discovers Victor Sterling — the man who actually holds it — is genuinely bad, and takes him down for her friends.

Core line:
> Some empires are built on fear. Hers was built on favors.

## Character arc
- **Act 1 — Small Favors:** Parmida chases the title. Sterling is background noise on invoices.
- **Act 2 — The Squeeze:** she meets the real thing and hates the mirror. Sterling pushes back through systems, then makes it personal (he holds Ellie).
- **Act 3 — What He Can't Buy:** she takes him down, not for territory but for her friends, and redefines the title on her own terms.

## Pillars
- Friends helping friends is the main mechanic and the emotional thesis.
- Failure is progress: every bad job returns the player to the hideout with intel, dialogue, and a reason to try again.
- Compact systems should imply a bigger city: mission cards, polaroids, crew favors, trophies, and recurring contacts.
- Bentley is mechanically important: sniff trails, bark stun, loyalty saves, and comic judgment.
- The final heist should pay off every friend helped earlier — assists and ending lines scale with favors earned and Night Jobs completed.

## Tone
Stylish, playful, romantic, noir-flavored, action-thriller, funny through banter and specificity, sincere without becoming grimdark.

## Night Jobs (side track, canonical)
Small optional adventures (5–10 minutes) with their own micro-stories, named NPCs, and **unique** minigames/puzzles never reused from story missions. They build Parmida's street legend and reward scheme cards, collectible set pieces, hideout flair, and rumor dialogue:
- Corner Store Cashout (first Night Job, tutorial-scale)
- The Laundromat Heist (planned; blueprint exists)
- Arm-Wrestling Underground (Violet's repeatable minigame venue)
- Bentley's Walk (planned scent-trail minigame)
- Hideout rehearsal puzzles (safe-dial listening, Polaroid darkroom, tea-brewing memory)
- Future "friendship jobs" — one per crew member post-recruitment

## Key mechanics notes
- **Cool-Down Shift is a mechanic, not a mission:** replay a completed mission with blend-in objectives ("act believable") to lower that venue's heat before the next job.
- **The conspiracy must be player-visible:** every story mission wires one Sterling clue into the evidence board with connecting copy.
- **Sterling has presence between missions:** escalation beats after Rewrite Room (rival's welcome note) and Fast Family Getaway (personal retaliation, Ellie reveal).
